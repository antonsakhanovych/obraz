# Obraz

*From Russian образ, the complete inner form of a person made visible. Not a portrait. The full truth.*

*The questions are the starting point. Your answers, and everything beyond them that is true, are the framework. Write Signal last.*

---

## Signal

*Who you are before the doing starts, the irreducible self and the thing it is transmitting. This is the sentence you return to when you are lost. Write it last, after everything else is filled.*

**Who are you when everything is stripped away, no projects, no roles, no relationships present to define you? What remains?**

**What is the single sentence that orients everything you do? What are you trying to transmit, and to whom?**

---

## Problems

*Not inconveniences. Things that bother you morally or intellectually, that keep returning even when no one is asking you to care. These are the wounds that became lenses. Remove anything inherited rather than felt.*

**What is the most broken thing in the world that you personally feel drawn to address? Not what you think you should care about, what actually keeps returning.**

**What broken thing do you encounter most in daily life, in how people relate, in institutions, in systems? The thing you notice that others seem not to.**

**What broken thing did you experience personally, not abstractly, that formed how you see the world?**

---

## Maxims

*Not aspirational values. Principles already governing your behavior, that you would hold even when they cost you something. For each one ask: would I universalize this? Does my actual behavior match it? If a maxim has never cost you anything it may not be real yet.*

**State a principle you have actually acted on at personal cost. What is it and what did it cost you?**

**What do you believe that most people around you do not, and would not change under social pressure?**

**What principle governs how you treat people who can do nothing for you?**

---

## Frequency

*Not who you know, the wavelength you operate on. What genuine contact feels like, what you offer, what you are looking for, and how you distinguish it from noise. This section should be uncomfortable to write. If it is easy you are being generic.*

**What is your definition of love? Not what you have been told, what you actually believe based on how you have experienced it.**

**What is your definition of friendship? How does it differ from love?**

**What do you offer the people closest to you, specifically, not generically? Not "I am loyal." What does your loyalty look like in practice?**

**What are you looking for in another person that you have not yet found? Be honest about what is rare, not what sounds good.**

**What does genuine contact feel like, and how do you distinguish it from its absence?**

---

*Obraz has no terminal state. It is not a plan toward a finished version of yourself. Revisit it when you make a significant decision, when something breaks, or when you feel you have lost the thread.*

*Signal should stay stable. Everything else should change.*
