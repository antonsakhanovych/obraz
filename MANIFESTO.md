# Obraz

*From the Russian образ: the complete inner form of a person, made visible. It is meant to hold the whole truth, more than a portrait would.*

*Start with the questions. The framework is your answers plus anything else true about you that the questions didn't ask for. Write Signal last.*

## Signal

*Who you are before you start doing anything: the irreducible self and what it transmits. This is the sentence you come back to when you are lost, so write it last, once everything else is filled in.*

- Who are you with everything stripped away, when no projects, roles, or relationships are there to define you? What remains?
- What single sentence orients everything you do? What are you trying to transmit, and to whom?

## Problems

*Things that bother you morally or intellectually and keep coming back even when nobody asks you to care. Mere inconveniences don't belong here. These are the wounds that became lenses. Cut anything you inherited rather than felt.*

- What is the most broken thing in the world that you personally feel drawn to fix? Not what you think you should care about: what actually keeps coming back.
- What broken thing do you run into most in daily life, in how people relate to each other or in institutions and systems? Look for something you notice that others seem to miss.
- What broken thing did you live through firsthand, rather than in the abstract, that shaped how you see the world?

## Maxims

*Principles that already govern how you behave and that you would hold even when they cost you something. Aspirations don't count. For each one, ask whether you would universalize it and whether your actual behavior matches it. A maxim that has never cost you anything may not be real yet.*

- State a principle you have acted on at a personal cost. What is it, and what did it cost you?
- What do you believe that most people around you don't, and would keep believing under social pressure?
- What principle governs how you treat people who can do nothing for you?

## Frequency

*The wavelength you operate on, as opposed to who you happen to know. It covers what real contact feels like to you, what you offer, what you are looking for, and how you tell it apart from noise. This section should be uncomfortable to write. If it comes easily, you are being generic.*

- What is your definition of love? Not what you have been told: what you actually believe, based on how you have experienced it.
- What is your definition of friendship, and how does it differ from love?
- What do you specifically offer the people closest to you? "I am loyal" is too generic; describe what your loyalty looks like in practice.
- What are you looking for in another person that you have not found yet? Be honest about what is rare, not about what sounds good.
- What does genuine contact feel like, and how do you tell when it's missing?

---

*Obraz has no end state, and it does not lead toward a finished version of you. Come back to it when you make a big decision, when something breaks, or when you feel you have lost the thread.*

*Signal should stay stable. Everything else should change.*
